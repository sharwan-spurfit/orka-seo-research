## SERP read (top 10, India, desktop)
- marathon training plan: AI Overview first, then Hal Higdon, Nike, Runner's World, YouTube, a clinic, Cancer Research UK, GU, HOKA PDF. Strong brands, no India-specific page.
- pace calculator: calculator.net, active.com, Strava, McMillan, calculatorsoup, Finalsurge, Play Store app. Tool pages rank; Strava is a competitor here.
- running app: Strava (Play, App Store, site), Runkeeper, adidas, YouTube, listicles. Dominated by big apps.
- best running app: listicles (weeviews, jamestrodgers, barbend), YouTube, Strava, and one India page (thehigh5tribe.com, "Best Apps for Runners in India").
- mumbai marathon: BookMyShow, worldsmarathons, racemart.in, ahotu, mahamarathon, marathonmitra. Event/registration aggregators, no training content.
- delhi half marathon: AIMS, ahotu, Facebook, Athletics Federation of India, Shutterstock, Instagram. Weak, non-optimised pages.
- half marathon training plan: Injinji, Mottiv, Team RWB, On, PureGym, Runna. Brand and shoe-company blogs.
- 10k training plan: Decathlon Coach, Team RWB, RNLI PDF, Brooks, Marathon Handbook, ASICS.
- zone 2 running: Juniper, Peloton, Sunny Health, YouTube, Runner's World UK. Mixed blogs.
- how to build running endurance: Peloton, strengthrunning.com, Kickoff, Chalk Talk, Perpetua, Gymshark. Weak/blog-level pages.

## Implications (for the scoring phase)
- Highest opportunity: India race/event queries (high volume, low KD, weak SERPs) paired with a race training plan; marathon/half/10k plan pages; pace calculator and marathon pace calculator as tool pages; how to run faster / build endurance / start running as guides.
- Competitive: shoes (high advertiser competition), generic "running app" (Strava and big apps).
- Positioning only: AI coach, adaptive plan, HRV, missed-training. Low volume now; keep for later and brand terms.
