# Final findings (India, English, 21 Oct 2026)

## Seasonality (OpenSEO monthly volumes, Oct 2025 - Sep 2026)
- Marathon plan pool (18,100 average): about 1,900 (Oct), 2,400 (Nov), a one-month spike to 110,000 (Dec 2025), 5,400 (Jan), 27,100 (Feb and Mar), then 2,900-5,400 (Apr-Aug) and 14,800 (Sep 2026). Demand clusters in Dec-Mar and starts rising from Sep. The marathon page should be live and indexed before December.
- 10K plan: peak 5,400 in Dec 2025 (average 1,775). 5K plan: peak 1,600 in Dec. Half marathon plan: peak 5,400 around Apr/Aug 2026.
- Delhi Half Marathon peaks in Oct; Mumbai, Pune and Bengaluru marathon searches peak around Jan.
- "How to start running" shows a one-month spike (14,800 in Sep 2026), probably an event; treat its 2,400 average with caution.
- Google Trends was not used.

## Page inspection (top results for the marathon plan pool)
- Hal Higdon: 14 plans, static, miles only, no heat or India content, paid interactive version through TrainingPeaks.
- Nike: 18 weeks, the plan lives in the Nike Run Club app, only 2 weeks visible on the page, no numeric paces, no heat or India content.
- On India: 16-week generic plan, miles only, all easy/long/recovery runs (no tempo or intervals), no heat or India adjustments.
- Common gaps: kilometres, heat and humidity, pollution, working-week schedules, goal-time paces, what to do after a missed week.

## Keyword gap (at least 2 competitors rank, at least one at positions 4-20, 1,000+ searches)
54 keywords (data/keyword_gap_2plus_competitors.csv). Most are variants in the marathon, 10K and 5K plan pools. Variants within a pool return the same Hal Higdon, Nike and On pages, so each pool is one page. "How to run marathon" (Hal Higdon 4, Marathon Handbook 10, Runna 20) returns different pages than the plan pool, which supports a separate guide.
