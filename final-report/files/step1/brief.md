# Research brief

- Product: running and marathon training plans.
- Market: India, English.
- Goal: qualified organic visitors who install the app.
- Principle: start from the market and search demand, not from the website's existing content.

## Rules set before collecting data
- Score each keyword cluster 1-5 on: product relevance, search demand, intent fit, competition ease, ranking opportunity, business value. Confidence is rated separately.
- Four weightings are tested to check the order is stable.
- Volume gate: a page's primary keyword must sit in a volume pool of at least 1,000 searches a month. Below that it becomes a section of another page or brand copy.
- Variants Google reports with the same volume are one pool and are never added together.
- Tiers: 1 = score 4.0 or more, 2 = 3.5-3.99, 3 = 3.0-3.49, 4 = below 3.0.
- Data source: OpenSEO (DataForSEO), India, English.
